## ❓ What is Criminal-Appeal?

Criminal-Appeal is a tool designed to help users manage and analyze legal cases related to murder appeals under NSW State and Australian Federal law. 

With this app, you can:

- Organize case documents in one place  
- Build clear timelines for events  
- Generate AI-powered legal reports with grounds of merit and references  
- Produce Barrister-style professional reports ready for court presentations  

The app aims to simplify the often complex process of tracking and reviewing criminal appeal cases.

## 📂 Organizing Your Case Files

Once installed, start by creating a new case in the app. Here’s how:

- Open Criminal-Appeal and click **New Case**.  
- Enter case details such as the case name, appeal type, and involved parties.  
- Use the **Document Upload** feature to add relevant files like statements, evidence, and transcripts.  

Keep your files organized in folders that the app creates automatically. This helps in quick access and better analysis.

## 🕰️ Building a Timeline

A clear timeline is key to understanding case events. Use the **Timeline Builder** in Criminal-Appeal:

- Add events with dates and descriptions.  
- Attach relevant documents or notes to each event.  
- Rearrange items if needed to get the correct sequence.  

The timeline view makes it easy to follow what happened and when during the appeal.
